New
- A new + in the iPhone chat: it opens a panel in the keyboard's place with the newest photos and videos, the camera, and Photos, Files and Scan buttons. Tapping a photo adds it to the message at once, numbered; tapping it again takes it out. Tapping the text brings the keyboard back, and × closes the panel.
- Document scan: the scanned pages are sent as one PDF.
- With limited photo access, the end of the row has a tile to choose more photos.

Please try: open + in a chat, pick a few photos and send them, take one out of the message, take a photo and scan a document.
