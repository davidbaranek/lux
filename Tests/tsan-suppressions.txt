# Thread Sanitizer suppressions for `swift test --sanitize=thread` (.github/workflows/ci.yml).
#
# UniFFI's async calls (Vendor/LuxBindings, generated): Swift suspends in withUnsafeContinuation,
# and `uniffiFutureContinuationCallback` resumes the continuation once the Rust future is ready,
# at times before the task has reached the await. The task then goes on in place, and TSan sees
# the resume and the read after it unordered: the Swift runtime gives the sanitizer no acquire on
# that path, and it reports a race that is not one. It depends on timing: often on the runner
# (3 cores, macOS 27.0), rarely on a fast Mac (once in some 15 runs, under load, on macOS 27.3).
# The line goes once Swift's runtime tells the sanitizer about that acquire. Only that callback:
# a race anywhere else in the bindings or in LuxCore is still reported.
race:uniffiFutureContinuationCallback
